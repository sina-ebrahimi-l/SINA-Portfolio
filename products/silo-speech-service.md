# SILO Speech Service

## Overview

A shared speech-processing layer for the SINA/SILO ecosystem.

It turns voice and audio into reusable production assets for transcription, subtitles, post-production assistance and voice-driven product workflows.

## The Problem

Speech tasks were previously repeated across separate tools and often required manual correction, timing work and format conversion.

## The Solution

SILO Speech Service centralizes those capabilities into one internal service that can be reused by content workflows and product interfaces.

## Public Capabilities

- Persian-focused speech transcription
- Word-level timing data
- Subtitle generation
- Audio cleanup and rough-cut assistance
- Timeline-aware subtitle regeneration after editing
- Voice/file intake through a lightweight operator interface
- Reusable integration path for internal products

## Product Role

The service is designed as shared infrastructure rather than a one-off transcription tool. It can support content production, journal voice input, internal assistants and future media workflows.
