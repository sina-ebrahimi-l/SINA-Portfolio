# SIA — AI Business Copilot

## Overview

SIA is an AI-assisted operational copilot designed to connect customer relationship management, sales processes, payment records and administrative workflows.

## The Problem

Customer information, sales conversations, payments, contracts and follow-ups often become fragmented across messaging platforms and separate tools.

This creates duplicate records, incomplete customer histories and unnecessary manual work.

## The Solution

SIA provides a conversational interface for managing these workflows while keeping CRM and accounting records synchronized.

## Core Capabilities

- Lead intake and customer profiling
- New and returning customer identification
- Sales and service history management
- Payment confirmation and evidence storage
- Subscription and renewal tracking
- Follow-up reminders
- Contract and invoice workflows
- Connection between CRM and accounting records
- AI-assisted operational actions

## My Role

Product strategy, workflow architecture, business logic, operational design, quality control and development coordination.
