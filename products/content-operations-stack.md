# Content Operations Stack

## Overview

A connected operations layer for publishing, scheduling, newsroom work, live distribution and audience-response workflows.

## System Family

- Content Distribution System
- Community Publisher
- Community Newsroom
- Live Orchestrator
- Instagram Automation
- SINA Deep Link

## Operating Model

The stack separates preparation, approval, publishing and platform-specific execution so that content can move through repeatable workflows without forcing every service to know how every destination works.

## What This Demonstrates

- Multi-system orchestration
- Operator approval before high-impact actions
- Reusable platform adapters
- Scheduled and event-driven workflows
- Cross-channel reporting
- Separation between public content logic and private platform credentials

## Public Boundary

The public portfolio documents responsibilities and outcomes, not private platform rules, exact automation logic, account configuration or deployment details.
