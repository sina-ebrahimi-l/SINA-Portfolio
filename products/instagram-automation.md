# Instagram Automation

## Overview

A dedicated automation infrastructure for managing publishing and engagement workflows across selected Instagram accounts.

## The Problem

Different brands and content categories require separate publishing rules. Generic automation can accidentally publish content to the wrong account or mix unrelated audience workflows.

## The Solution

The system uses dedicated publishing routes and explicit account rules for each content category.

## Core Capabilities

- Automated Instagram Story publishing
- Explicit account selection
- Brand-specific publishing flows
- Keyword-based audience interaction
- Automated direct-message handoff
- Publishing health checks
- Delivery and failure reporting
- Integration with content-management workflows

## My Role

System requirements, account-flow architecture, automation rules, risk management, testing and development supervision.
